Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

ViewCast helps YouTube creators understand their channel performance. This policy explains what information the service uses and the choices available to you.

Information we process

When you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier, display name, and sign-in metadata. If you connect YouTube, ViewCast requests read-only access to your channel identity, public video information, YouTube Analytics reports, and—when available—monetary analytics.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to provide visible creator features such as dashboards, video analysis, retention reporting, audience discovery, and revenue metrics. ViewCast does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Storage and sharing

Your ViewCast profile and linked-channel summary are stored in Firebase under your account identifier. Short-lived Google access tokens and detailed analytics caches are stored in your browser. Google user data is not shared with third parties except service providers required to operate features you request, such as Firebase and Google APIs, or when required by law.

Google API Services

ViewCast's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your controls and deletion

You can unlink YouTube from My Channel, which revokes the current Google access token and removes locally cached creator data. You can permanently delete your ViewCast account and saved profile from Settings. You can also revoke ViewCast through your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Security and retention

ViewCast uses HTTPS, Firebase security rules, Firebase App Check, owner-only data access, and limited-duration browser caches. Information is retained only while needed to provide the service or until you unlink or delete your account, subject to required operational and legal retention.
